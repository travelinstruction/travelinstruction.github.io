# Booking a bus ticket with the TmoneyGO app

**TmoneyGO (티머니GO)** is the app that sells the express and intercity bus tickets used in this
guide — including the airport buses from Daejeon to Incheon Airport (ICN) and from Yuseong to
Cheongju Airport (CJJ). Buying in the app is the most reliable way to secure a seat, because the
early-morning airport buses are often sold out on the day of travel.

This page is written for **a foreigner who holds a Korean credit or check (debit) card**.
The app has **no English interface**, so every screen below is annotated: the red box shows what
to tap, and the Korean words you need are spelled out.

{: .warning}
The app registers **only cards issued in Korea and in your own name**. A card issued outside Korea
will not register, and some Korean cards are blocked by their issuer. If you do not have a Korean
card, ask your host at IBS to buy the ticket for you and send you the QR-code ticket, or buy your
ticket at the machine in the bus stop.

{: .note}
You need to install **TmoneyGO** ([iOS](https://apps.apple.com/kr/app/id1447934505) ·
[Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tmoney.tmoneygo)) and sign in before
you start. Signing up requires a Korean mobile phone number for the identity check, so do this
while you still have your Korean SIM.

<h2 id="step1">Step 1 — Open the app and choose 공항버스 (Airport bus)</h2>

On the home screen, stay on the first tab **교통** (Transport) and tap **공항버스** — the third icon
in the top row, the one with a purple bus.

{: .note}
Going somewhere that is not an airport (Seoul, Busan…)? Tap **고속·시외** instead, the icon to its
left. Everything after this step works the same way.

The app opens with an advertisement or a notice. Tap the black **닫기** (Close) button. The white

way to find your stop is the region tabs: tap **대전/충남** (Daejeon / South Chungcheong), then
scroll the list until the Korean name matches the one you are looking for.

Rather than scrolling, use the grey strip of letters down the right edge — it is a Korean

and **대전청사 / 대전청사공항(선사유적)** are the Government Complex stops. The one nearest IBS is
**대전도룡**.

<h2 id="step4">Step 4 — Set 도착지, where you get off</h2>

Now tap **도착지** (Destination). For the airport bus the list is short, because it only shows
airports. Pick **인천공항T1** or **인천공항T2** according to your flight — or **청주공항** for
Cheongju.

<h2 id="step5">Step 5 — Pick the date and the earliest hour</h2>

Tap **가는날** (Date of travel). Choose the day from the calendar, then choose the earliest
departure hour you want to see from the row of chips below — **07시** means "07:00 and later".
Grey chips are hours that have already passed. Finish with the black **선택완료** button.

{: .warning}
The airport buses to Incheon start running around 03:00. A bus that leaves at 03:14 leaves on the
**calendar day of your flight**, not the evening before — pick the date carefully, and set the
hour chip to **00시** so that the night departures are not hidden.

that the two stop names are the right way round — the ⇄ button swaps them — and that the date is
correct. Then tap the black **버스 조회** button.

<h2 id="step7">Step 7 — Dismiss the operator notice</h2>

A notice from the bus company appears. Tap **확인** (OK). It is worth knowing what it says: you may
bring **two pieces of luggage per person**, and a third piece can be charged at 50% of an adult
fare.

<h2 id="step8">Step 8 — Choose your departure</h2>

Each card is one bus: the operator, the departure time, the journey time (**약 2시간 53분** =
about 2 h 53 min), and the fare. The tags at the bottom right tell you whether you can still book:

- **매진** — sold out, in red. You cannot take this bus.
- **1석 남음** — 1 seat left. **9석 남음** — 9 seats left.
- **심야우등 / 심야프리미엄** — late-night deluxe / premium class. Fewer, wider seats; premium
  costs more. Both are comfortable.
- **경유** — this bus makes a stop on the way.

Tap anywhere on a card that is not sold out.

<h2 id="step9">Step 9 — Pick your seat</h2>

The seat map opens. **전체 28석** is the total number of seats and **잔여 N석** is how many are
free. Seats marked **✕** are taken; tap a free seat to select it, and tap it again to release it.
Tap one seat per traveller.

The chips **일반 / 아동 / 중고생 / 보훈** set the fare type — adult, child, middle or high school
student, and veteran. **Leave it on 일반 (Adult)**; the others require Korean ID to prove
eligibility.

When your seat turns purple, the total appears at the bottom. Tap the black **선택완료**.

<h2 id="step10">Step 10 — Check the reservation and tap 결제하기 (Pay)</h2>

This screen summarises the journey: date and time, operator and class, **출발** (from), **도착**
(to), **좌석** (seat number) and **운임** (fare). Read it once more, then tap **결제하기** (Pay).

{: .warning}
**결제기한** is your payment deadline — the seat is only held until then. The notice also warns
that the reservation is cancelled if you close the app, and that it expires 10 minutes before
departure for an intercity bus. Do not leave the app between here and the end of Step 12.

<h2 id="step11">Step 11 — Choose 신용/체크 등록 간편결제 as the payment method</h2>

Scroll past **쿠폰 및 GO마일리지** (coupons and points — leave both alone) down to **결제수단**,
the payment methods. Ignore Mobile Tmoney, N Pay, toss pay and KB Pay: those need separate Korean
accounts. Select the last option, **신용/체크 등록 간편결제** — "pay with a registered credit or
check card" — and tap the dashed **+** box below it to add your card.

<h2 id="step12">Step 12 — Register your card</h2>

The camera opens to read the card automatically: hold the card inside the yellow frame. If it will
not focus — or if your card has no embossed numbers — tap the black **직접입력** button at the
bottom and type the details instead. You will be asked for the card number, the expiry date, the
first two digits of your card PIN, and your date of birth in **YYMMDD** form.

The two lines under the frame are the app's warnings: register a card **issued in your own name**,
and some cards are blocked by their issuer.

{% include app_screenshot.html
   src="/assets/images/tmoneygo/tmoney-go-15.jpeg"
   alt="Card scanning screen with the manual entry button highlighted"
   boxes="3,88,94,5,1"
   caption="Hold the card in the yellow frame, or tap 직접입력 to type the numbers yourself." %}

Once the card is registered you return to the payment screen. Tap the black **결제하기** at the
bottom one last time, and the purchase is complete.

<h2 id="ticket">After paying — finding and reading your ticket</h2>

There is no paper ticket. Your ticket lives in the app, on the same booking screen you started
from: scroll down to **예약 현황** (Reservation status). The number beside it is how many tickets
you are holding. **이용내역**, to its right, is your past purchase history.

Tap the card — it shows the route, the date and the number of passengers — to open the ticket
itself.

The first time you open a ticket, an illustrated **이용 가이드** (usage guide) pops up over it. It
has two pages and shows the one thing that matters: hold your phone's QR code against the reader
just inside the bus door. Tap **확인** to close it — **다시 보지 않기** above the button means
"don't show this again".

Reading the rest of it:

| Korean | Meaning |
| :-- | :-- |
| 출발 / 도착 | From / to. A name in brackets under 도착, such as **(대전복합발)**, is where the bus started its run — not your stop. |
| 출발일 / 시간 | Departure date / time |
| 승차홈 | Boarding platform. **현장확인** means "check the signs when you get there" — common at small stops. |
| 좌석번호 | Your seat number |
| 예상소요시간 | Estimated journey time |
| 등급 | Class — 우등 (deluxe), 일반 (standard) |
| 운행회사 | The bus company, printed on the side of the bus |
| 승차권저장 | Save the ticket to your phone |
| 여정 정보 | Trip details |
| 실시간 위치 | Live bus location — greyed out until close to departure |

{: .note}
Tap **승차권저장** (or take a screenshot) before you leave for the bus stop. The stops near IBS have
poor mobile reception, and a saved image works even when the app will not load.

{: .warning}
Be at the stop **10 minutes early** and check the sign on the front of the bus. Buses to Seoul and
other cities use the same stops, and the buses leave exactly on the minute printed on your ticket.

<h2 id="change">Changing or cancelling a ticket</h2>

Open the ticket as above and scroll to the bottom. Below the fare and the payment details are two
buttons: **예매변경** (change the booking) and **예매취소** (cancel it). **예매변경** reopens the
search for the same route so you can move to another departure, subject to free seats;
**예매취소** refunds the ticket, minus the fee in the table below. The app shows you the refund
amount before you confirm, so you can always back out.

**영수증**, in the **결제상세** (payment details) box above, is your receipt — useful if you are
claiming travel expenses.

You can only do this from **the account that bought the ticket**. If a colleague at IBS booked for
you, ask them to make the change — it is not in your app.

Once the QR code has been scanned you can no longer cancel in the app; the guide pop-up says to
ask the driver or the ticket window instead.

<h3 id="fees">Cancellation fees</h3>

Intercity buses charge a cancellation fee that grows as departure approaches. This is the official
schedule that took effect on 1 May 2025, as a percentage of the fare:

| When you cancel | Bus departs Mon–Thu | Bus departs Fri–Sun or a public holiday | Seollal / Chuseok period |
| :-- | :--: | :--: | :--: |
| 2 or more days before departure | **0%** | **0%** | **0%** |
| 1 day to 3 hours before | 5% | 7.5% | 10% |
| Under 3 hours before, up to departure time | 10% | 15% | 20% |
| Up to 1 hour after departure | 40% | 40% | 40% |
| 1 to 4 hours after departure | 60% | 60% | 60% |
| More than 4 hours after departure | 100% — no refund | 100% | 100% |

There is also a grace period: cancelling **within one hour of booking** is free, as long as the bus
has not yet left. Fees are rounded down to the nearest 100 won.

{: .note}
The 1-to-4-hours-after rate rises over time — it was 50% in 2025, is **60% through 30 April 2027**,
and becomes 70% after that. The fee is unchanged for the airport limousine operators
(공항리무진, 도심공항리무진, K공항리무진, 서울공항리무진), which are excluded from this schedule.

Source: [시외버스 취소수수료 변경 안내](https://txbus.t-money.co.kr/intro/notice_20250425.html),
the official intercity bus reservation system. Flying out of Korea for good? Cancel as soon as your
plans change — two days ahead it costs you nothing.

<h2 id="trouble">If something goes wrong</h2>

- **The card will not register.** It is almost certainly issued outside Korea, or not in your own
  name. Ask a colleague at IBS to book for you and send you the QR code — a ticket bought on
  someone else's account is still valid for you.
- **Everything is 매진 (sold out).** Try the **유성복합 → 인천공항T1/T2** route instead. Yuseong is a
  different terminal with its own departures, so it often still has seats when Doryong is full. Go
  back to Step 3 and set 출발지 to **유성복합**.
- **You left the app mid-booking.** The held seat is gone. Start again from Step 6; the seat is
  usually still free for a few minutes.
